Batch 1 · Willow Fields creatures

Side and front blueprints for the six Willow Fields creatures, drawn to their in-game size (grid = 1 stud). Semi-realistic, smooth and high-poly (no studs, no low-poly facets). Each model is split into rig pieces (Body, Head, Leg ×4, Tail, glow parts) so it can be animated. Glowing parts are separate so they can pulse. Approve or mark changes, then I model the batch in Blender.
